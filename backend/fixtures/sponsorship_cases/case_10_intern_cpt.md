## Machine Learning Intern, Applied Research
NVIDIA — Santa Clara, CA / Boston, MA

Join NVIDIA's Applied Research team as a summer intern working on GPU-accelerated ML systems.

### What you'll do
- Prototype new sparsity / quantization primitives on H100 and B200 GPUs.
- Benchmark inference kernels against internal transformer models.
- Present a public technical demo at end-of-internship.

### Who we're looking for
- Currently enrolled in a Master's or PhD program in CS, ECE, or a related field.
- Deep familiarity with PyTorch and CUDA fundamentals.

### Eligibility
NVIDIA supports internships for candidates on F-1 CPT and F-1 OPT status. International students in a US degree program are welcome to apply — we work with candidates to secure the appropriate work authorization for the internship period.
