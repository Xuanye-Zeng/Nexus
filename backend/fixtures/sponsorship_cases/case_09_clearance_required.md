## Cloud Systems Engineer, National Security

Amazon Web Services is expanding its National Security team. Work on classified compute infrastructure for U.S. Government customers.

### Responsibilities
- Design + operate isolated AWS regions on classified fabrics.
- Automate compliance controls (FedRAMP High, IL5, IL6).
- Support incident response and continuous ATO.

### Required
- 5+ years of cloud infrastructure engineering.
- Active TS/SCI clearance with polygraph is required at time of application.
- Must be a US citizen — this role supports U.S. Government classified programs and non-US citizens cannot be considered.

### Preferred
- Experience with Kubernetes and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, CDK).
- Familiarity with air-gapped operations and cross-domain solutions.
